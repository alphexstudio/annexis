About Annexis

Mission Matters.

Mission Matters. People Matters.

Turn scholarly metadata metrics into actionable insight: every number must show what to improve and how.

No metric without an action layer; no action layer without an open, evidence-backed path to repair.

01

Measure

Make the missing metadata signal visible through a transparent diagnosis.

02

Act

Turn each gap into a specific recommendation and an ordered next step.

03

Repair

Develop evidence-backed fixer support that can close the gap at its source.

Nexus Fix is currently in R&D.
