Good questions

What Nexus Index is and is not.

Clear boundaries make the diagnostic more useful: it measures the record’s reuse readiness, not the prestige or quality of the research.

Metadata readiness≠research quality

It reflects how complete and machine-readable a record’s metadata is across five facets: Provenance, People, Organizations, Funding, and Access. In short, how ready the record is for discovery, attribution, and reuse by people and AI.

No. It measures the record’s reuse readiness, not the prestige or quality of the research. Metadata readiness and research quality are different things.

A record is reusable when its links support attribution, access, and responsible reuse, so that both humans and AI agents can find it, credit it, and build on it.

It is informed by FAIR Data and FORCE11 Data Citation principles. These are guiding references, not standards or certification.

Institutions & libraries, publishers, AI companies, and service providers can work with it now. Experiences for researchers and funders are coming soon.

No. Nexus API checks reuse readiness at the point of use, and Nexus MCP keeps agent interactions traceable. The underlying gaps still need to be repaired, ideally once at the substrate so the fix flows everywhere downstream.

Why Annexis exists

AI cannot attribute what the record does not connect.

Our 2026 attribution study tested the mechanism directly. When a metadata connection was hidden, the related task failed. Restoring the matching connection recovered it. When People metadata stayed hidden, extra search and compute produced varied outcomes but no fully correct author link rescue. It also added recurring inference cost.
